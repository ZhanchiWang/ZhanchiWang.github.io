About

I am Zhanchi Wang, an Associate Research Fellow at the Institute of Artificial General Intelligence, University of Science and Technology of China (USTC). My research focuses on soft robotics, embodied intelligence, and physical human–robot interaction, with the goal of enabling robots to interact with the physical world efficiently, autonomously, and safely.

I received both my bachelor's degree and Ph.D. from USTC, completing my Ph.D. in 2025.

王展翅，中国科学技术大学通用人工智能研究所特任副研究员。聚焦于柔性机器人、具身智能与人机物理交互等前沿方向，致力于让机器人与物理世界进行高效、自主、安全的交互。本科和博士均毕业于中国科学技术大学，2025 年获博士学位。

Open Positions / 招生与招聘

I welcome undergraduate research interns, prospective master's and Ph.D. students, and engineers interested in soft robotics, embodied intelligence, and physical human–robot interaction. If you are interested, please email your CV and representative work to zhanchi@ustc.edu.cn. You are also welcome to contact me directly to discuss opportunities.

欢迎对柔性机器人、具身智能与人机物理交互感兴趣的本科实习生、硕士生、博士生及工程师加入。如有意向，请将简历和代表性工作发送至 zhanchi@ustc.edu.cn，欢迎直接邮件联系我交流。
