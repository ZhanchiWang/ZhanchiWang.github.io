Hi, I'm **Zhanchi Wang**. I study soft robotics — machines built from compliant materials that move gently, adapt to the world, and feel a little more alive.

I enjoy the full loop: **design → fabrication → experiments**. My favorite projects are the ones that bend, twist, and surprise people with simple, elegant motion.

If you'd like to collaborate, swap ideas, or compare notes on soft robots, feel free to email me.

### What I care about
- Soft actuation and smart materials
- Embodied intelligence and morphological design
- Human-friendly interaction and playful demos
