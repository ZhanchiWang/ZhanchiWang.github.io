About

I study soft robotics: how compliant bodies move, adapt, and interact with the world. I am interested in the design, modeling, and control of soft robots, especially spiral-shaped robots for grasping and manipulation.

I like robots that bend the rules—and themselves. For research conversations and collaborations, feel free to get in touch.
